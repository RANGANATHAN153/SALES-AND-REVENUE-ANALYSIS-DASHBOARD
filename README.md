# SALES-AND-REVENUE-ANALYSIS-DASHBOARD
Built a Sales &amp; Revenue Analysis Dashboard in Power BI using Excel data to track sales, profit, revenue trends, and top-performing products through interactive visualizations and KPI analysis.

This project was developed to analyze business sales performance and convert raw sales data into meaningful insights using Excel and Power BI. The main objective of the project was to understand how different regions, products, customer types, and payment methods contribute to overall revenue and profit generation.

We first created a structured sales dataset in Excel containing important business attributes such as Order ID, Order Date, Region, Salesperson, Product Category, Product Name, Units Sold, Unit Price, Revenue, Cost, Profit, Customer Type, and Payment Mode. The dataset was designed to simulate real-world business transactions with hundreds of records to make the analysis realistic and useful.

After preparing the dataset, the Excel file was imported into Power BI for data visualization and reporting. Inside Power BI, the data was cleaned and validated to ensure consistency and remove possible errors. We then used DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) to create important business KPIs such as Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Cost, Profit Margin Percentage, Average Revenue, and Total Units Sold. These measures helped in calculating business performance dynamically based on filters and slicers.

The dashboard was designed with multiple interactive visualizations to provide a complete view of the business performance:

* KPI Cards were used to display overall business metrics like total sales, total profit, and profit margin.
* A Clustered Column Chart was created to compare revenue generated across different regions.
* A Bar Chart was used to analyze profit contribution by product category.
* A Line Chart displayed monthly sales trends to identify growth patterns and seasonal changes.
* A Treemap visualized top-performing products based on sales revenue.
* A Pie Chart analyzed customer payment preferences such as UPI, Cash, and Card transactions.
* Slicers were added for Region, Product Category, Customer Type, and Date to make the dashboard fully interactive.

Through this analysis, the dashboard helps identify:

* Which region generates the highest revenue
* Which product category produces maximum profit
* Best-selling products
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Preferred payment methods
* Monthly business growth trends

Overall, this project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, business analysis, DAX calculations, data visualization, dashboard design, and transforming raw data into actionable business insights for decision-making.
